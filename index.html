FAQ

Common questions.

What is the difference between licensing and custom collection?

Licensing uses data hospitals already hold, so it is faster. Custom collection creates new data to your protocol. Many programs combine both.

Do you only work in neurology and pathology?

No. Those are our delivered programs so far. Our partner hospitals cover most specialties, including cardiology, radiology, ophthalmology and dermatology.

Can the data meet FDA, HIPAA and UK requirements?

Yes. We build datasets to FDA standards, deidentify to HIPAA standards, and handle processing and transfer in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Can you annotate data we already have?

Yes. Share your data under a data agreement and our specialists will label it to your guidelines.

How quickly can we get data?

Feasibility in one to two weeks. Licensed data usually ships within weeks of approval. Custom timelines are agreed up front.

Can we evaluate a sample first?

Yes. For most licensed data we can share a small sample before you commit.

Is the data exclusive?

Licences are shared by default. Exclusive terms are available, and custom data can be yours alone.

Do you work with smaller teams?

Yes. We work with startups, research groups and large companies, and can scope small pilot datasets.
