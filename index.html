What we do

Two ways to get the data your model is missing.

Whether a suitable archive already exists or the dataset has to be created from scratch, we handle the hospital relationships, approvals and logistics — you get model-ready data.

Licensing & access

Retrospective data licensing

We match your requirements to archives held by partner hospitals, then broker a licence on clear terms. Fastest route to volume for pre-training, validation and benchmarking.

  • Feasibility check across the network, typically within days
  • De-identified at source before anything leaves the hospital
  • Licence terms covering use, exclusivity and retention
  • Sample sets available for evaluation before you commit
Custom collection

Purpose-built datasets

When the data doesn't exist yet, we design and run a collection program across partner centres — to your protocol, devices, labels and demographic targets.

  • Protocol design with clinicians and your ML team

  • Expert annotation by practising specialists

FAQ

Common questions.

What's the difference between licensing and custom collection?

Licensing gives you access to data hospitals already hold — it's faster and suited to volume. Custom collection creates new data to your protocol, which suits specific devices, labels, rare conditions or prospective study designs. Many programs combine both.

How quickly can we get data?

We usually confirm feasibility within one to two weeks. Retrospective deliveries typically begin within weeks of approvals; custom collection timelines depend on volume and recruitment and are agreed up front.

Can we evaluate a sample first?

Yes. For most retrospective programs we can arrange a small sample set so your team can check format, quality and labels before committing to a full order.

Is the data exclusive?

By default licences are non-exclusive. Exclusive or time-limited exclusive terms can be negotiated, and data from custom collection programs can be built exclusively for you.

What formats do you deliver in?

Native clinical formats where possible — EDF/EDF+ for EEG, DICOM for imaging, SVS/TIFF for whole-slide images — plus structured metadata in CSV or JSON. We can map to your schema on request.

Do you work with academic groups and startups?

Yes. We work with teams of all sizes and can scope smaller pilot datasets for early-stage programs.
